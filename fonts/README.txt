KFGQPC HAFS Uthmanic Script, version 18
=======================================

The typeface of the Madinah Mushaf, made by the King Fahd Glorious Quran
Printing Complex (KFGQPC), al-Madinah al-Munawwarah. Every verse of the
Quran on this site is drawn with it.

  UthmanicHafs_V18.woff2   - what browsers load
  UthmanicHafs_V18.ttf     - the same font, for a browser without woff2

Both files are byte-identical to the copies quran.com serves:
  https://quran.com/fonts/quran/hafs/uthmanic_hafs/UthmanicHafs1Ver18.woff2

  https://quran.com/fonts/quran/hafs/uthmanic_hafs/UthmanicHafs1Ver18.ttf

LICENCE (it is embedded in the font itself, name record 13): KFGQPC grants
anyone, free of cost, the right to use, copy and distribute the font. It may
not be sold, modified, altered, translated, reverse-engineered or
decompiled. So: never subset it, never re-save it through a font tool, never
convert it. Replace it only with another unmodified release from KFGQPC.

WHY THIS FONT AND NO OTHER. The Quran text in js/quran-text.js is KFGQPC's
own Hafs text, and that text encodes the open (staggered) tanween with three
code points - U+0657, U+065E, U+0656 - that every other font draws as a
different mark. In any other typeface those verses show the wrong vowel.
