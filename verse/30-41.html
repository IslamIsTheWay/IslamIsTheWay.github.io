Ar-Rum 30:41 — explained simplyشرح الآية ٤١ من سورة الروم

﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾

“Corruption has appeared throughout the land and sea by [reason of] what the hands of people have earned so He may let them taste part of [the consequence of] what they have done that perhaps they will return [to righteousness].”

💬 In plain wordsبكلامٍ بسيط

The damage on land and sea is laid at people's own hands — and then two words turn it into mercy: they are made to taste only some of what they did, and so that perhaps they will return. The purpose is return, not ruin.

خرابُ اليابسة والبحار يُردّ إلى ما صنعه الناس، ثم تأتي كلمتان تجعلانه رحمة: يُذيقهم بعضَ ما عملوا لا كلَّه، لعلّهم يرجعون. فالغايةُ الرجوعُ لا الهلاك.

Corruption has appeared on land and sea because of what people's hands have earned. The verse makes the damage visible — zahara, it has come out into the open — and gives it one cause: what people themselves have done.

Then two words turn the sentence into a mercy. Ba'd — so that He lets them taste some of what they did; not all of it. And la'allahum yarji'un — so that perhaps they will return. The taste is measured, and it has a purpose: return, not ruin.

Ash-Shura says how much is held back: whatever strikes you is from what your hands have earned, and He pardons much (42:30). Fatir says what full accounting would mean: if Allah took people to task for all they earned, He would not leave a single creature on the face of the earth (35:45). And Al-A'raf gives the command that comes before all of it: do not cause corruption on the earth after it has been set right (7:56).

﴿ظَهَرَ ٱلۡفَسَادُ فِي ٱلۡبَرِّ وَٱلۡبَحۡرِ بِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِي ٱلنَّاسِ﴾: الآيةُ تجعل الفسادَ ظاهرًا مكشوفًا، وتردّه إلى سببٍ واحد: ما صنعه الناسُ بأنفسهم.

ثم كلمتان تقلبان الجملةَ رحمة: ﴿لِيُذِيقَهُم بَعۡضَ ٱلَّذِي عَمِلُواْ﴾ — البعضَ لا الكلّ — و﴿لَعَلَّهُمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ﴾. فالذَّوقُ بمقدار، وله غاية: الرجوعُ لا الهلاك.

والشورى تقول كم يُمسك عنهم: ﴿وَمَآ أَصَٰبَكُم مِّن مُّصِيبَةٖ فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾، وفاطرٌ تقول ماذا يعني الحسابُ التامّ: ﴿وَلَوۡ يُؤَاخِذُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلنَّاسَ بِمَا كَسَبُواْ مَا تَرَكَ عَلَىٰ ظَهۡرِهَا مِن دَآبَّةٖ﴾. والأعرافُ تذكر الأمرَ الذي يسبق ذلك كلَّه: ﴿وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَا﴾.

Surah Ar-Rum (30:41), read with Ash-Shura (42:30), Fatir (35:45) and Al-A'raf (7:56)

And whatever strikes you of disaster - it is for what your hands have earned; but He pardons much.
Surah Ash-Shuraa (42:30)

Ar-Rum says people taste only some of what they did; Ash-Shura says how much is held back: whatever strikes you is from what your hands earned — and He pardons much.

الرومُ تقول إنهم يذوقون بعضَ ما عملوا، والشورى تقول كم يُمسك عنهم: ﴿فَبِمَا كَسَبَتۡ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَيَعۡفُواْ عَن كَثِيرٖ﴾.

And if Allah were to impose blame on the people for what they have earned, He would not leave upon the earth any creature. But He defers them for a specified term. And when their time comes, then indeed Allah has ever been, of His servants, Seeing.
Surah Fatir (35:45)

Fatir says what a full accounting would mean: if Allah took people to task for all they earned, He would not leave a single creature on the earth.

And cause not corruption upon the earth after its reformation. And invoke Him in fear and aspiration. Indeed, the mercy of Allah is near to the doers of good.
Surah Al-A'raf (7:56)

Al-A'raf gives the command behind it all: do not cause corruption on the earth after it has been set right — and call on Him in fear and hope.

الأعرافُ تذكر الأمرَ الذي وراء ذلك كلّه: ﴿وَلَا تُفۡسِدُواْ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ بَعۡدَ إِصۡلَٰحِهَا وَٱدۡعُوهُ خَوۡفٗا وَطَمَعًاۚ﴾.

🌍 From The earth, animals and the environment🌍 من باب الأرض والحيوان والبيئة

The verse ties the harm we see — droughts, lost blessing, damaged land and sea — to what people do. Turning back begins with you: your sins, and your waste.

تربط الآية ما نراه من ضرر، كالجفاف وذهاب البركة وتلف البرّ والبحر، بأعمال الناس. والرجوع يبدأ منك: من ذنوبك، ومن تبذيرك.
