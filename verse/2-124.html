Al-Baqarah 2:124 — explained simplyشرح الآية ١٢٤ من سورة البقرة

﴿۞ وَإِذِ ٱبۡتَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِـۧمَ رَبُّهُۥ بِكَلِمَٰتٖ فَأَتَمَّهُنَّۖ قَالَ إِنِّي جَاعِلُكَ لِلنَّاسِ إِمَامٗاۖ قَالَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۖ قَالَ لَا يَنَالُ عَهۡدِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ﴾

“And [mention, O Muhammad], when Abraham was tried by his Lord with commands and he fulfilled them. [Allah] said, "Indeed, I will make you a leader for the people." [Abraham] said, "And of my descendants?" [Allah] said, "My covenant does not include the wrongdoers."”

💬 In plain wordsبكلامٍ بسيط

Ibrahim fulfilled every test and was made a leader for people — and when he asked the same for his descendants, the answer named a condition: My covenant does not reach the wrongdoers. Leadership in religion is not inherited; it is earned again in each generation.

أتمّ إبراهيمُ كلَّ ابتلاء فجُعل للناس إمامًا، وحين سأل ذلك لذرّيّته جاء الجوابُ بشرط: العهدُ لا يناله ظالم. فالإمامةُ في الدين لا تُورَث، بل تُكتسب من جديدٍ في كلّ جيل.

Ibrahim was tested by his Lord with words — commands — and he fulfilled them. The commentators differed over what they were: the rites of the Hajj, said some; the practices of natural cleanliness, said others; the trials of his life — the fire, the emigration, the son — said others. The verse does not list them. It says only that he completed them: fa-atammahunn.

Then the reward: I will make you a leader — imam — for people. And at once Ibrahim asks it for his descendants: and of my offspring? The answer neither grants nor refuses the family; it names a condition: My covenant does not reach the wrongdoers. Leadership in religion is not inherited. It is earned again in each generation, and lost by wrongdoing.

Two verses later Ibrahim, having heard that, prays for the city's provision only for the believers among its people — and Allah widens it to the disbelievers too, for a little while (2:126). Bread is for everyone; the covenant is not. As-Saffat shows one of the tests in full: the dream, the son, and the words 'this was the clear trial' (37:102-106).

﴿وَإِذِ ٱبۡتَلَىٰٓ إِبۡرَٰهِـۧمَ رَبُّهُۥ بِكَلِمَٰتٖ فَأَتَمَّهُنَّۖ﴾. واختلف المفسّرون في هذه الكلمات: فقيل مناسكُ الحجّ، وقيل خصالُ الفطرة، وقيل محنُ حياته — النارُ والهجرةُ والابن. والآيةُ لا تعدّها، بل تقول إنه أتمّها.

ثم الجزاء: ﴿قَالَ إِنِّي جَاعِلُكَ لِلنَّاسِ إِمَامٗاۖ﴾. وفي الحال يسأله إبراهيمُ لذرّيّته: ﴿قَالَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِيۖ﴾، فلا يُعطى البيتُ ولا يُمنع، بل يُذكر شرط: ﴿قَالَ لَا يَنَالُ عَهۡدِي ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ﴾. فالإمامةُ في الدين لا تُورَث، بل تُكتسب من جديدٍ في كلّ جيل، وتُفقد بالظلم.

وبعد آيتين يدعو إبراهيمُ — وقد سمع ذلك — برزق البلد للمؤمنين من أهله فقط، فيوسّعه الله حتى على من كفر، متاعًا قليلًا. فالخبزُ للجميع، والعهدُ ليس كذلك. والصافّاتُ تُريك أحدَ الابتلاءات كاملًا: الرؤيا، والابن، ثم ﴿إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَهُوَ ٱلۡبَلَٰٓؤُاْ ٱلۡمُبِينُ﴾.

And [mention] when Abraham said, "My Lord, make this a secure city and provide its people with fruits - whoever of them believes in Allah and the Last Day." [Allah] said. "And whoever disbelieves - I will grant him enjoyment for a little; then I will force him to the punishment of the Fire, and wretched is the destination."
Surah Al-Baqarah (2:126)

Two verses later Ibrahim, having heard that the covenant does not reach wrongdoers, limits his prayer for provision to the believers — and Allah widens it to everyone.

وبعد آيتين يقصر إبراهيمُ — وقد سمع أنّ العهد لا يناله الظالم — دعاءَه بالرزق على المؤمنين، فيوسّعه الله على الجميع: ﴿قَالَ وَمَن كَفَرَ فَأُمَتِّعُهُۥ قَلِيلٗا﴾.

And when he reached with him [the age of] exertion, he said, "O my son, indeed I have seen in a dream that I [must] sacrifice you, so see what you think." He said, "O my father, do as you are commanded. You will find me, if Allah wills, of the steadfast."
Surah As-Saffat (37:102)

As-Saffat shows one of the tests he fulfilled, in full: the dream, the son who said 'do as you are commanded', and the clear trial.

Indeed, Abraham was a [comprehensive] leader, devoutly obedient to Allah, inclining toward truth, and he was not of those who associate others with Allah.
Surah An-Nahl (16:120)

An-Nahl gives what the fulfilling made of him: Ibrahim was a nation in himself, devoutly obedient to Allah, inclining to the truth.

النحلُ تذكر ما صنعه به الإتمام: ﴿إِنَّ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ كَانَ أُمَّةٗ قَانِتٗا لِّلَّهِ حَنِيفٗا﴾.
