At-Tawbah 9:100 — explained simplyشرح الآية ١٠٠ من سورة التوبة

﴿وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ وَأَعَدَّ لَهُمۡ جَنَّٰتٖ تَجۡرِي تَحۡتَهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُ خَٰلِدِينَ فِيهَآ أَبَدٗاۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡعَظِيمُ﴾

“And the first forerunners [in the faith] among the Muhajireen and the Ansar and those who followed them with good conduct - Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him, and He has prepared for them gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever. That is the great attainment.”

💬 In plain wordsبكلامٍ بسيط

The first emigrants and Ansar — and then anyone after them who follows them in excellence: the door stays open, with a condition. And the pleasure goes both ways: Allah is pleased with them and they with Him, words the Quran uses in only four places.

المهاجرون والأنصارُ الأوّلون، ثم كلُّ من جاء بعدهم فاتّبعهم بإحسان — فالبابُ مفتوحٌ بشرط. والرضا من الجهتين: ﴿رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ﴾، وهي عبارةٌ لم ترد في القرآن إلا في أربعة مواضع.

Three groups, and the third is where everyone after them is invited in. The first forerunners from the Muhajirun — those who left Makkah — and the Ansar — those who took them in at Madinah — and then: those who followed them in excellence. The door does not close with the generation that saw the Prophet ﷺ. It is open to anyone, on one condition — ihsan: following them well, not merely claiming them.

Who the first forerunners were, the commentators differed: those who prayed towards both qiblahs, said some; the people of Badr, said others; those of the pledge under the tree, said others. Each answer marks a moment when believing cost something.

Then the verse says something the Quran says in only four places: Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him (5:119, 9:100, 58:22, 98:8). It runs both ways. He accepted what they gave, and they accepted what He gave — including what was hard.

Al-Hashr names the same three groups in three consecutive verses — the emigrants (59:8), the Ansar (59:9), and those who came after them (59:10) — and gives the third group its words: our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith.

ثلاثُ فِرَق، والثالثةُ هي البابُ الذي يدخل منه كلُّ من جاء بعدهم: ﴿وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلۡأَوَّلُونَ مِنَ ٱلۡمُهَٰجِرِينَ وَٱلۡأَنصَارِ﴾ — مَن هاجروا من مكّة، ومَن آوَوهم في المدينة — ثم: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ﴾. فالبابُ لا يُغلق بالجيل الذي رأى النبيَّ ﷺ، بل هو مفتوحٌ لكلّ أحدٍ بشرطٍ واحد: الإحسان، أي أن يتّبعهم اتّباعًا حسنًا، لا أن ينتسب إليهم دعوى.

واختلف المفسّرون في السابقين الأوّلين: فقيل هم الذين صلّوا إلى القبلتين، وقيل أهلُ بدر، وقيل أهلُ بيعة الرضوان تحت الشجرة. وكلُّ قولٍ منها يعلّم لحظةً كان الإيمانُ فيها يُكلّف شيئًا.

ثم تقول الآيةُ ما لم يقله القرآنُ إلا في أربعة مواضع: ﴿رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ﴾ (المائدة ١١٩، والتوبة ١٠٠، والمجادلة ٢٢، والبيّنة ٨). فالرضا من الجهتين: قَبِل منهم ما قدّموا، وقبلوا منه ما أعطى، ومنه ما كان شاقًّا.

والحشرُ تذكر الفِرقَ الثلاث نفسها في ثلاث آياتٍ متتالية: المهاجرين، ثم الأنصار، ثم الذين جاؤوا من بعدهم، وتعطي الفرقةَ الثالثة كلماتِها: ﴿رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ﴾.

Surah At-Tawbah (9:100), read with Al-Hashr (59:8-10); the four places of mutual pleasure: 5:119, 9:100, 58:22, 98:8; who the forerunners were: Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 9:100

Their reward with Allah will be gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever, Allah being pleased with them and they with Him. That is for whoever has feared his Lord.
Surah Al-Bayyinah (98:8)

At-Tawbah says it of the first generation and those who follow them in excellence; Al-Bayyinah says it of all who believe and do good: Allah is pleased with them and they with Him — and adds the condition: that is for whoever fears his Lord.

التوبةُ تقولها في الجيل الأوّل ومن اتّبعهم بإحسان، والبيّنةُ تقولها في كلّ مؤمنٍ عاملٍ للصالحات، وتزيد الشرط: ﴿ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنۡ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُۥ﴾.

And [also for] those who were settled in al-Madinah and [adopted] the faith before them. They love those who emigrated to them and find not any want in their breasts of what the emigrants were given but give [them] preference over themselves, even though they are in privation. And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul - it is those who will be the successful.
Surah Al-Hashr (59:9)

Al-Hashr shows the Ansar of this verse in action: they loved those who emigrated to them, and preferred them over themselves even in their own need.

And [there is a share for] those who came after them, saying, "Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith and put not in our hearts [any] resentment toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind and Merciful."
Surah Al-Hashr (59:10)

The third group's own words: our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and put no rancour in our hearts towards those who believed.
