Al-Baqarah 2:282 — the Verse of Debt — explained simplyشرح الآية ٢٨٢ من سورة البقرة — آية الدَّين

﴿يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيۡنٍ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٖ مُّسَمّٗى فَٱكۡتُبُوهُۚ وَلۡيَكۡتُب بَّيۡنَكُمۡ كَاتِبُۢ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ وَلَا يَأۡبَ كَاتِبٌ أَن يَكۡتُبَ كَمَا عَلَّمَهُ ٱللَّهُۚ فَلۡيَكۡتُبۡ وَلۡيُمۡلِلِ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ وَلۡيَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُۥ وَلَا يَبۡخَسۡ مِنۡهُ شَيۡـٔٗاۚ فَإِن كَانَ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ سَفِيهًا أَوۡ ضَعِيفًا أَوۡ لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُ أَن يُمِلَّ هُوَ فَلۡيُمۡلِلۡ وَلِيُّهُۥ بِٱلۡعَدۡلِۚ وَٱسۡتَشۡهِدُواْ شَهِيدَيۡنِ مِن رِّجَالِكُمۡۖ فَإِن لَّمۡ يَكُونَا رَجُلَيۡنِ فَرَجُلٞ وَٱمۡرَأَتَانِ مِمَّن تَرۡضَوۡنَ مِنَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءِ أَن تَضِلَّ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا فَتُذَكِّرَ إِحۡدَىٰهُمَا ٱلۡأُخۡرَىٰۚ وَلَا يَأۡبَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءُ إِذَا مَا دُعُواْۚ وَلَا تَسۡـَٔمُوٓاْ أَن تَكۡتُبُوهُ صَغِيرًا أَوۡ كَبِيرًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلِهِۦۚ ذَٰلِكُمۡ أَقۡسَطُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقۡوَمُ لِلشَّهَٰدَةِ وَأَدۡنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَرۡتَابُوٓاْ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ تِجَٰرَةً حَاضِرَةٗ تُدِيرُونَهَا بَيۡنَكُمۡ فَلَيۡسَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ جُنَاحٌ أَلَّا تَكۡتُبُوهَاۗ وَأَشۡهِدُوٓاْ إِذَا تَبَايَعۡتُمۡۚ وَلَا يُضَآرَّ كَاتِبٞ وَلَا شَهِيدٞۚ وَإِن تَفۡعَلُواْ فَإِنَّهُۥ فُسُوقُۢ بِكُمۡۗ وَٱتَّقُواْ ٱللَّهَۖ وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ ٱللَّهُۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيۡءٍ عَلِيمٞ﴾

“O you who have believed, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down. And let a scribe write [it] between you in justice. Let no scribe refuse to write as Allah has taught him. So let him write and let the one who has the obligation dictate. And let him fear Allah, his Lord, and not leave anything out of it. But if the one who has the obligation is of limited understanding or weak or unable to dictate himself, then let his guardian dictate in justice. And bring to witness two witnesses from among your men. And if there are not two men [available], then a man and two women from those whom you accept as witnesses - so that if one of the women errs, then the other can remind her. And let not the witnesses refuse when they are called upon. And do not be [too] weary to write it, whether it is small or large, for its [specified] term. That is more just in the sight of Allah and stronger as evidence and more likely to prevent doubt between you, except when it is an immediate transaction which you conduct among yourselves. For [then] there is no blame upon you if you do not write it. And take witnesses when you conclude a contract. Let no scribe be harmed or any witness. For if you do so, indeed, it is [grave] disobedience in you. And fear Allah. And Allah teaches you. And Allah is Knowing of all things.”

💬 In plain wordsبكلامٍ بسيط

Write down what you lend and what you owe — even between brothers, even a small amount, even when you trust each other. The longest verse in the Quran is a contract, and the reason it gives is not suspicion: it is so that you will not end up doubting one another.

اكتب ما أقرضتَ وما عليك — ولو بين إخوة، ولو كان قليلًا، ولو كان بينكما ثقة. فأطولُ آيةٍ في القرآن عقدٌ مكتوب، وعلّتُها ليست سوء الظنّ، بل ألّا تنتهيا إلى الارتياب.

This is the longest verse in the Quran. It is not about prayer, or fasting, or the hereafter. It is about writing down a loan.

Read what it insists on. Write it. Let a scribe write it justly. Let the one who owes dictate — so the record is in the debtor's own words — and let him fear Allah and not diminish anything of it. If he cannot dictate, his guardian dictates justly. Two witnesses. Do not be too weary to write it, small or large, to its term. That is more just with Allah, more upright for testimony, and nearer to your not doubting each other.

A Book that spends its longest verse on paperwork is telling you what it thinks of people's money — and of the quarrels that start when nothing was written down.

هذه أطولُ آيةٍ في القرآن، وليست في صلاةٍ ولا صيامٍ ولا آخرة، وإنما في كتابة دَين.

وانظر فيمَ شدّدت: اكتبوه، وليكتب بينكم كاتبٌ بالعدل، ﴿وَلۡيُمۡلِلِ ٱلَّذِي عَلَيۡهِ ٱلۡحَقُّ﴾ — فيكون السجلُّ بلفظ المَدين نفسِه — وليتّق الله ربّه ولا يبخس منه شيئًا. فإن كان لا يستطيع أن يُملّ فليُملل وليُّه بالعدل. وشهيدان. ﴿وَلَا تَسۡـَٔمُوٓاْ أَن تَكۡتُبُوهُ صَغِيرًا أَوۡ كَبِيرًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلِهِۦۚ﴾. ذلكم أقسطُ عند الله وأقومُ للشهادة و﴿وَأَدۡنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَرۡتَابُوٓاْ﴾.

وكتابٌ يُنفق أطولَ آيةٍ فيه على التوثيق يقول لك كيف ينظر إلى أموال الناس — وإلى الخصومات التي تبدأ حين لا يُكتب شيء.

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:282)
The Quranقرآن
تَدَايَنتُمtadayantum — you deal in debt with one another

Form VI again — the form for something done between two parties. Not “when you lend” and not “when you borrow”, but when the two of you enter a debt together. The whole shape of the verse follows from that word: it protects both sides at once, and it gives the pen to the weaker one.

على وزن «تفاعَل» — بناءُ ما يقع بين اثنين. فليست «إذا أقرضتم» ولا «إذا استدنتم»، بل إذا دخلتما في الدَّين جميعًا. ومن هذه الكلمة جاءت صورةُ الآية كلِّها: تحفظ الطرفين معًا، وتجعل الإملاء لمن عليه الحقّ.

Arabic morphology (form VI, tafāʿul, for a mutual act)
الصرف العربي (صيغة «تفاعَل» لفعلٍ بين طرفين)
Language — from a named lexiconلغة — من معجمٍ مسمّى
تَسۡـَٔمُوٓاْla tas'amu — do not grow weary

The verse names the real reason people skip the writing, and it is not dishonesty. It is tiredness — the loan is small, we know each other, we will remember. Small or large, says the verse, to its term.

سمّت الآيةُ السببَ الحقيقيّ لترك الكتابة، وليس هو الخيانة، وإنما السآمة: المبلغُ يسير، ونحن نعرف بعضنا، وسنذكر. فقالت: صغيرًا أو كبيرًا إلى أجله.

وَإِن كُنتُمۡ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٖ وَلَمۡ تَجِدُواْ كَاتِبٗا فَرِهَٰنٞ مَّقۡبُوضَةٞۖ
“And if you are on a journey and cannot find a scribe, then a pledge taken in hand.”
Surah Al-Baqarah (2:283)

The exception that shows how serious the rule is: when writing is impossible, something else must stand in its place — and the verse then goes straight to the case where the two do trust each other, and tells the trusted one to fear Allah and discharge the trust.

الاستثناءُ يدلّ على شدّة الأصل: فإذا تعذّرت الكتابةُ قام غيرُها مقامها — ثم انتقلت الآيةُ إلى حال الائتمان، فأمرت المؤتمَن أن يتّقي الله وأن يؤدّي الأمانة.

🤲 From Giving, lending and doing favours🤲 من باب العطاء والدَّين والمعروف

Write down every loan, even between brothers and close friends. It is not distrust — it is the Quran's way of protecting the relationship from forgetting and suspicion.

اكتب كلّ قرض، ولو بين إخوةٍ وأصدقاءَ مقرّبين. فهذا ليس شكًّا، بل طريقة القرآن في حماية العلاقة من النسيان وسوء الظنّ.
