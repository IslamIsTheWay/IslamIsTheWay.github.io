Al-Hashr 59:10 — explained simplyشرح الآية ١٠ من سورة الحشر

﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ رَءُوفٞ رَّحِيمٌ﴾

“And [there is a share for] those who came after them, saying, "Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith and put not in our hearts [any] resentment toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind and Merciful."”

💬 In plain wordsبكلامٍ بسيط

Those who came after the first Muslims are described by a prayer, not a deed: forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith, and put no rancour in our hearts towards those who believed. 'A'ishah said people were commanded to ask forgiveness for the Companions, and reviled them instead (Muslim 3022).

من جاؤوا بعد المسلمين الأوائل يُوصفون بدعاءٍ لا بعمل: أن يغفر لهم ربُّهم ولإخوانهم السابقين، وألّا يجعل في قلوبهم ضغينةً على المؤمنين. وقالت عائشةُ إنّ الناس أُمروا أن يستغفروا للصحابة فسبّوهم (مسلم ٣٠٢٢).

Al-Hashr names three groups in three verses: the emigrants who left their homes and wealth (59:8), the Ansar who loved those who came to them (59:9), and then everyone after: those who came after them. Their description is not a deed; it is a prayer. They are known by what they say about the ones before them.

Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith. They ask for themselves and for people they never met in one breath, and call them brothers. Then: and put no ghill in our hearts towards those who believed. Ghill is the rancour that settles inside unseen — the same word used for what Allah removes from the hearts of the people of Paradise before they sit as brothers on couches facing one another (15:47).

'A'ishah said to her nephew 'Urwah: people were commanded to ask forgiveness for the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, and they reviled them (Muslim 3022). This verse is that command — the way a later Muslim is meant to speak of the first ones.

تذكر الحشرُ ثلاثَ فِرَقٍ في ثلاث آيات: المهاجرين الذين تركوا بيوتَهم وأموالهم، والأنصارَ الذين أحبّوا المهاجرين، ثم كلَّ من جاء بعدهم: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ جَآءُو مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِمۡ يَقُولُونَ﴾. ووصفُهم ليس عملًا بل دعاء، فهم يُعرفون بما يقولونه عمّن سبقهم.

﴿رَبَّنَا ٱغۡفِرۡ لَنَا وَلِإِخۡوَٰنِنَا ٱلَّذِينَ سَبَقُونَا بِٱلۡإِيمَٰنِ﴾: يسألون لأنفسهم ولقومٍ لم يلقوهم في نَفَسٍ واحد، ويسمّونهم إخوة. ثم: ﴿وَلَا تَجۡعَلۡ فِي قُلُوبِنَا غِلّٗا لِّلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ﴾. والغِلّ الضغينةُ التي تستقرّ في الداخل خفيّة، وهو اللفظُ نفسه لما ينزعه الله من صدور أهل الجنّة قبل أن يجلسوا إخوةً متقابلين: ﴿وَنَزَعۡنَا مَا فِي صُدُورِهِم مِّنۡ غِلٍّ إِخۡوَٰنًا عَلَىٰ سُرُرٖ مُّتَقَٰبِلِينَ﴾.

وقالت عائشةُ لابن أختها عروة: أُمروا أن يستغفروا لأصحاب النبيّ ﷺ فسبّوهم (مسلم ٣٠٢٢). وهذه الآيةُ هي ذلك الأمر، والطريقةُ التي يُراد أن يتكلّم بها المسلمُ المتأخّر عن الأوّلين.

Surah Al-Hashr (59:10), read with 59:8-9 and Al-Hijr (15:47); commanded to ask forgiveness for the Companions: Sahih Muslim 3022, from 'A'ishah
سورة الحشر، الآية ١٠، مع الآيتين ٨–٩ وسورة الحجر ٤٧؛ والأمرُ بالاستغفار للصحابة: صحيح مسلم ٣٠٢٢، عن عائشة رضي الله عنها

And the first forerunners [in the faith] among the Muhajireen and the Ansar and those who followed them with good conduct - Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him, and He has prepared for them gardens beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever. That is the great attainment.
Surah At-Tawbah (9:100)

At-Tawbah names the same three groups — the emigrants, the Ansar, and those who followed them in excellence; Al-Hashr gives the third group its words.

التوبةُ تذكر الفِرقَ الثلاث نفسها — المهاجرين والأنصار والتابعين بإحسان — والحشرُ تعطي الفرقةَ الثالثة كلماتِها: ﴿وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّبَعُوهُم بِإِحۡسَٰنٖ رَّضِيَ ٱللَّهُ عَنۡهُمۡ وَرَضُواْ عَنۡهُ﴾.

The believers are but brothers, so make settlement between your brothers. And fear Allah that you may receive mercy.
Surah Al-Hujurat (49:10)

Al-Hujurat says believers are brothers; Al-Hashr carries the brotherhood across the generations: our brothers who preceded us in faith.

And We will remove whatever is in their breasts of resentment, [so they will be] brothers, on thrones facing each other.
Surah Al-Hijr (15:47)

Al-Hijr uses the same word for what Allah removes in Paradise: We will remove whatever rancour is in their breasts — brothers, on couches facing one another.

And [also for] those who were settled in al-Madinah and [adopted] the faith before them. They love those who emigrated to them and find not any want in their breasts of what the emigrants were given but give [them] preference over themselves, even though they are in privation. And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul - it is those who will be the successful.
Surah Al-Hashr (59:9)

The verse before is the second group: the Ansar, who loved those who emigrated to them and preferred them over themselves.

Clean your heart of grudges against other Muslims, past and present — and make this verse your du'a, especially when you hear people curse the Companions or other Muslims.

طهّر قلبك من الحقد على المسلمين، السابقين والحاضرين، واجعل هذه الآية دعاءك، خاصّةً حين تسمع من يسبّ الصحابة أو غيرهم من المسلمين.
